# LANDFILL-SITE-SELECTION-USING-GIS-IN-ILORIN-METROPOLIS
The aim of this project is to determine Land suitability for landfill site in Ilorin metropolis, Kwara State, for the disposal of solid wastes in the most efficient location.
The objectives of this study are;
1.	To generate landfill criteria maps of the study area.
2.	To integrate MCDM (multi criteria decision making) and GIS in the study area for the purpose of landfill site selection.
3.	To determine potential landfill sites in the study area.
Study Area:
Ilorin is the largest metropolitan settlement in Kwara State, situated in the North Central geopolitical zone of Nigeria. Geographically, the town lies within 08° 10’00” N and 04° `10’00” E, with an approximate area of 2,687 per square Kilometer, in accordance with the new master plan of Kwara State a buffer of 25 kilometer radius is buffered from the city’s epicenter (Post Office). Modern Ilorin is mainly inhabited by Muslim Yoruba people, although its traditional ruler is a Yoruba-speaking Fulani emir. The city expands across six coterminous local government administrative areas (Asa, Moro, Ifelodun, Ilorin West, Ilorin East, and Ilorin South). The current metro area population of Ilorin in 2022 is 1,000,000, a 2.67% increase from 2021, making it the seventh most populated city in Nigeria. In terms of climate, Ilorin has two seasons, wet and dry, the wet season occurs from April to October and dry season spans between November and April.  The modern Ilorin city is an industrial, commercial, and educational center. The growing industrial sector now includes sugar refining, food processing, soft-drink bottling, match and soap manufacturing, and ironworking. It is also a major market for locally produced crops such as yams, cassava, corn, sorghum, millet, rice, peppers, groundnut, shea nuts kola nuts, cotton, cattle, and poultry. Local handicrafts include pottery making, wood carving, leather working, cloth weaving and mat and basket weaving. There are several banks and insurance companies that serve the city and state. The city is home to more than two universities, the University of Ilorin and Al-Hikmah University with Kwara State University within its metropolitan area. Other institutions include Kwara State Polytechnic, Kwara State College of Education, Kwara State College of Arabic and Islamic Legal Studies, Emmanuel Baptist College, International Aviation College, Muhyideen college of education, the Federal Agricultural and Rural Management Training Institute (ARMTI),etc. vocational trade schools and more than 100 secondary schools (both private and public) also serve Ilorin.
The data used include:
 the location (point data) of the protected areas(mosques ,churches, and markets) and settlements, and also vector data (line) of the road network, water-course, other data used include digital elevation model (DEM), and Sentinel-2 resolution 10m(LULC), and Kwara state boundary shapefile (which include the study area), of which are accessible and gathered from different Open Datasets sources. Both the location data of the agricultural areas and the settlement were downloaded from the Gride3Nigeria (https://grid3.gov.ng/). Also, the road network data was also obtained from Gride3Nigeria (https://grid3.gov.ng/). Both the location Kwara state boundary shapefile and the Railway route  data was obtained from DIVA-GIS(Free Spatial Data | DIVA-GIS). While the surface slope was derived from the 30m resolution DEM downloaded from http://earthexplorer.usgs.gov, the DEM is a product of the Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) which represents the bare ground topography, the slope through the DEM was extracted.
